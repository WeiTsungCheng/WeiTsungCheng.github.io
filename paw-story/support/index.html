Frequently asked questions

Using Paw Story

Why can't Paw Story access my camera?

Camera access may have been disabled. Open the iOS Settings app, select Paw Story, and enable Camera access. You can also choose existing photos from your photo library instead.

Why can't I select a photo?

Try selecting the photo again or choosing another supported image. If the problem continues, close and reopen Paw Story.

Why do I need four photos?

Paw Story uses four photos to create the sequence of your story. The generated story follows the photos in the order they appear in the app.

Why is story generation taking a long time?

Story generation requires an internet connection and may take longer when the AI service is busy. Check your connection and try again after a short wait.

How can I share my story?

Open the generated story and tap the Share button. You can share the story as an image, as text, or as both image and text using the standard iOS sharing interface.

How can I save my story to Photos?

Open the generated story, tap the Share button, and select “Save Image”. Paw Story will create a story image containing your photos and generated text and save it to the Photos app.

Why can't I save the story image?

Paw Story needs permission to add the generated image to your photo library. Open iOS Settings, select Paw Story, and allow it to add photos.

How are my photos and story details handled?

Selected photos and story details are sent to Paw Story's service providers to provide AI-assisted features. For further information, read the Privacy Policy.

Why is AI-assisted input unavailable?

AI-assisted input requires iOS 26 or later, a compatible Apple Intelligence device, Apple Intelligence to be enabled, and the on-device model to be ready. You can continue filling out story preferences manually when the feature is unavailable.

Is my AI-assisted input sent to a server?

On supported devices, the raw text entered into AI-assisted input is processed on device. If you apply suggestions and later generate a story, the selected story details and photos are sent to Paw Story's backend and AI service providers to fulfil that request.
