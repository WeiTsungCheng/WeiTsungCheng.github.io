Frequently asked questions

Using Paw Story

Why can't Paw Story access my camera?

Camera access may have been disabled. Open the iOS Settings app, select Paw Story, and enable Camera access. You can also choose existing photos from your photo library instead.

Why can't I select a photo?

Make sure the selected file is a supported image and that Paw Story has permission to access the photo. Try selecting another image or restarting the app.

Why do I need four photos?

Paw Story uses four photos to create the sequence of your story. The generated story follows the photos in the order they appear in the app.

Why is story generation taking a long time?

Story generation requires an internet connection and may take longer when the AI service is busy. Check your connection and try again after a short wait.

Are breed suggestions always accurate?

No. Breed suggestions are generated with AI and are provided for informational purposes only. Results may be incomplete or inaccurate, particularly for mixed breeds or unclear photos.

Does Paw Story provide veterinary advice?

No. Paw Story is a creative storytelling app. Its photo descriptions and breed suggestions should not be treated as medical, behavioural or veterinary advice.

How are my photos and story details handled?

Selected photos and story details are sent to Paw Story's service providers to provide AI-assisted features. For further information, read the Privacy Policy.

How can I make a privacy or deletion request?

Email weitsung46@gmail.com with the subject “Paw Story Privacy Request”. Please do not attach private photos to the email.
