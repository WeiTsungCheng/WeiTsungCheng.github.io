Privacy Policy

Information We Process

Paw Story processes only the information you choose to provide when creating a story, identifying a dog breed, or analysing a dog photo. This may include:

  • Photos submitted for breed identification or dog photo analysis;
  • Breed suggestions, photo analysis results and generated stories returned in response to your request.
  • Photos selected from your device or taken with the camera;
  • Your dog's name, breed and optional description;
  • Story preferences such as language, audience, theme, tone, location, weather, keywords and additional notes;
  • The story generated in response to your request.

How We Use This Information

We use the information you provide to generate stories, identify possible dog breeds, analyse dog photos and save stories when you choose to do so. We do not use your photos or story information for advertising, user profiling or tracking.

On-Device AI-Assisted Input

On supported devices, Paw Story offers an optional AI-assisted input feature that helps organise a story idea into suggested story preferences.

Text entered into this feature is processed on the device using Apple's Foundation Models framework. Paw Story does not transmit or store the raw AI-assisted input as part of this feature.

Suggestions are shown for review and are applied only when you explicitly select them. If you later choose to generate a story, the selected story preferences may be sent to Paw Story's backend and AI service providers as described below.

AI Processing and Service Providers

When you request story generation, breed identification or photo analysis, Paw Story securely transmits your selected photos and relevant details, such as story preferences, to our backend service. The information may then be processed by third-party AI service providers solely to fulfil your request.

These providers process information according to their applicable service terms and privacy commitments. Paw Story does not sell your information or share it for advertising purposes.

Data Retention and Deletion

This information is processed only for the time required to generate and return your story. After the request is completed, Paw Story does not retain a personal copy of the submitted content on its application server.

Unsaved stories remain temporarily in the app while you use it. They are discarded when you reset or leave the story flow without saving.

When you choose “Save Story”, Paw Story stores the story on your device, including the generated text, selected photos, dog profile details, story preferences and creation date. Saved stories remain available after you close and reopen the app.

You can permanently remove a saved story from the app by opening it, tapping the trash button and confirming deletion. Copies exported to Photos, Files or other apps must be deleted separately.

Sharing Your Story

Paw Story may allow you to share a generated story using the standard iOS sharing interface. Sharing occurs only when you choose to use this feature and select a destination.

Once you share content with another app or service, that destination processes the shared content according to its own privacy policy. Paw Story does not control how the selected destination handles the content.

Paw Story allows you to save a generated story as an image in the Photos app. The story image is created on your device and saved only when you choose the “Save Image” option.

Paw Story requests add-only photo library access for this purpose. This permission allows Paw Story to add the generated image but does not allow it to browse your entire photo library.

Camera and Photo Access

Paw Story accesses the camera only when you choose to take a photo. Photos are accessed only when you select them for story generation, breed identification or photo analysis.

You can manage camera permission at any time in iOS Settings. Paw Story does not scan or access your entire photo library.

Children's Privacy

Paw Story is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should not submit photos containing identifiable people or include sensitive personal information in story notes.

Security

Paw Story uses encrypted HTTPS connections when transmitting information for story generation. However, no internet transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Paw Story's features or data practices change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
