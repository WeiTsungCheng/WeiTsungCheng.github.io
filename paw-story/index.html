How it works

From photos to story in four steps

  1. Add your dog Enter a name and optional breed information.
  2. Choose the style Select a theme, tone and target audience.
  3. Select four photos Arrange the moments in the order of your story.
  4. Keep or share the adventure Read your story, save it as an image, or share the image and text with other apps.

Privacy

Your photos deserve clear handling.

AI-assisted story input is processed on device on supported devices. When you choose to generate or analyse content, selected photos and story details are sent to Paw Story's backend and AI service providers to fulfil your request.
